01 / Architectural Foundations

[01.1]
Circadian Sleep & Rumination

The agent is not dormant when users disconnect. It executes an 8-phase daily metabolism: digesting raw conversational fragments into psychological tensions, dreams, and consolidated insights.

[01.2]
Thermodynamic Will Dynamics

Action is not triggered by user prompts alone. Three endogenous drives (Know, Relate, Express) accumulate pressure over time, discharging only through bounded actions and transactional receipts.

[01.3]
Evidence-First Autobiographical Memory

Eliminating narrative hallucination: every claim the agent makes regarding its past or identity must point to an immutable record identifier (e.g., conversation#1423, dream#87, loop#205).

[01.4]
Multi-Relational Cognitive Isolation

Structured in C12: each human bond forms an isolated relational boundary. Private tensions and memories remain strictly partitioned per relation, while only authorized aggregates inform global stance.

03 / Endogenous Will Dynamics

Most agents act only when prompted. JungAgent maintains a continuous drive pressure state. Action is framed as regulated discharge with idempotency, backoff, and refractory rest.

Drive 01 // Epistemic
Will to Know (Saber)

Rises from unresolved rumination, open knowledge gaps, and stale world context. Discharges through research, deep PDF assimilation, and synthesis.

Drive 02 // Relational
Will to Relate (Relacionar)

Rises from silence, lingering dialogic tensions, and interpersonal commitment. Discharges through bounded, consensual contact with specific relations.

Drive 03 // Symbolic
Will to Express (Expressar)

Rises from dream residue, mature insights, and creative overflow. Discharges through independent textual gestures, essays, and artistic works.
